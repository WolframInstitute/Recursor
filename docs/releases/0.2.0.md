# Recursor 0.2.0

Recursor now finds programs from the terms of a sequence, and its language
can apply one program inside another.

- `Quot[a, b]` gives an integer quotient, zero for a zero divisor.
- `Let[value, body]` evaluates a body with a new input. A closed program in
  the body is applied to the value, so programs compose into sums, products
  and searches. `Acc`, `Idx` and `CGet` keep their scoping.
- `SynthesizeRecursor[examples]` searches deterministically for the cheapest
  program that matches examples. It enumerates programs up to observational
  equivalence, checks recurrence steps against the data and solves for
  constants exactly: linear recurrences with polynomial coefficients,
  periodic increments, polynomials and constant tails.
- `RecursorExamples` adds Catalan, DigitSum, DivisorCount and Prime.
- The interpreter, the lambda compiler, the parser, the generators and the
  simplifier cover the new syntax. The Lean operational and denotational
  models include both constructs; the twelve checked theorems, including
  universality, hold for the extended language.
- A new guide and tutorial cover synthesis.

On the pinned OEIS snapshot of 283,167 eligible sequences, the corpus v2
experiment predicts the twelve terms after the first eight for 19,919
sequences, against 876 for the evolutionary search of corpus v1. From twenty
terms, 60,199 sequences have a program, and 33,506 of those programs
reproduce every later recorded term. See
[the experiment note](https://github.com/WolframInstitute/Recursor/blob/v0.2.0/docs/notes/OEISCorpusExperimentV2.md).

Programs written for 0.1.0 keep their meaning. Random generation now also
produces `Quot` and `Let`, so seeded random programs differ from 0.1.0.
Includes 67 documentation pages. Requires Wolfram Language 15.0 or later.
Released under the MIT license.

The formal theorems concern their specified models. Equivalence of the Wolfram
interpreter and lambda compiler to those models remains a separate proof
obligation. A program that reproduces every recorded term of a sequence is
evidence for a rule, not a proof of it. Execution budgets do not establish
nontermination.

The release contains the paclet, SHA-256 checksums, build provenance and the
PacletPage documentation build. Publication reuses the archive verified by CI.
